# Which old TutorPair Form ideas remain

**Kept and simplified**

- Professional tutor profile and evidence categories from legacy TutorRegistrationForm.
- Subject/grade + price + schedule structure from AddSubjectDialog, now split into **approved teaching scopes** and **freely published course listings**.
- Learning plans and checkbox-based objective completion from old Tutor Dashboard.
- Matching-engine concepts: hard filters, weighted score, explanations and top-three ranking.

**Removed from the new matching MVP**

- Nine student and tutor sliders: replaced with **three short shared questions** (pace, approach and interaction).
- Specific topic classification/selection during matching or course creation.
- Onsite location and travel radius; TutorPair is online-only.
- Repeat identity/credential review when creating courses in already-approved subject and grade scopes.

**Still planned, not implemented:** Real document verification, payment processing, LINE OA / LIFF connection, AI-assisted lesson plans and tutor/customer data from a secure database.
